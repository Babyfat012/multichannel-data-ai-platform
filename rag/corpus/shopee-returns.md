# Shopee — Return and Refund Policy (simulated)

## Return window
Buyers can request a return or refund within 15 days after the order is marked delivered, or until
they press "Order received", whichever comes first.

## Shopee Guarantee
Payment is held by Shopee until the buyer confirms receipt. During a dispute the money stays held,
so the seller is not paid until the dispute is closed.

## Dispute flow
The seller can accept, offer a partial refund, or dispute with evidence within 3 days. Shopee
decides disputes within 7 days.

## Common reasons
Wrong item sent, missing parts, damaged in transit, counterfeit claim. Damaged-in-transit claims
rise during big sale campaigns (9.9, 11.11, 12.12).

## Guidance
Pack fragile SKUs with extra protection before campaigns. Record a packing video for high-value
orders to win disputes.
