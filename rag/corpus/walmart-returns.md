# Walmart Marketplace — Return Policy (simulated)

## Return window
Buyers have 30 days from the delivered date. Walmart reports a "delivered" status to the seller,
so the window start is known exactly.

## Return methods
Buyers can mail the item back with a prepaid label or drop it at a store. Store drop-off returns
reach the seller later, often 7 to 14 days after the request.

## Refund timing
Refund is issued when the carrier scans the return label, before the seller inspects the item.
Shipping-only refunds (late delivery compensation) do not count as a product return.

## Seller-fulfilled vs Walmart-fulfilled
Seller-fulfilled orders follow the seller's return address. Walmart-fulfilled orders are handled
by the warehouse and the seller only receives the refund event.

## Guidance
A SKU with many approved returns in the last 24 hours may have a listing error (wrong colour or
wrong pack size). Pause the listing and compare the delivered item with the product page.
